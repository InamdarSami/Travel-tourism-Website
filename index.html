Travel Tips

Simple tips to make your India trip safe, smooth and memorable.

  • 📅

    Plan Your Trip

    Research destinations, book accommodations early and prepare a flexible itinerary.

  • 🎒

    Carry Essentials

    Pack comfortable clothes, medicines, travel documents and a reusable water bottle.

  • 🤝

    Respect Local Culture

    Dress modestly at religious sites and follow local customs and traditions.

  • 📞

    Keep Emergency Contacts

    Save local emergency numbers, embassy contacts and your hotel details.

  • 🌱

    Travel Responsibly

    Avoid littering, support local businesses and protect natural environments.
